How to Fix Ancestry Not Working
==========================================================
Ancestry not working can be a frustrating experience, especially when you are in the middle of important family history research.

When Ancestry is not working, the problem can affect the website, the mobile app, or both. This guide provides step-by-step troubleshooting solutions to resolve common issues, including pages not loading, login problems, app crashes, and display errors.

Understanding Ancestry Not Working
==================================

When Ancestry is not working, users typically encounter one of several scenarios. The website may display a blank page or show "pretty-print { }" instead of loading properly [citation:1]. The mobile app may crash, freeze, or fail to open [citation:2]. You may experience login issues, slow loading, or unexpected errors [citation:5][citation:6].

The Ancestry not working issue can stem from various sources, including IP address blocking, browser problems, cache and cookie issues, security software interference, or device compatibility problems. Understanding the specific symptom helps narrow down the cause and apply the right solution.

Check for Service Outages
=========================

Before troubleshooting your own device, check whether Ancestry is experiencing a service outage. Visit the Ancestry status page to determine if there are any known service issues [citation:5]. If there is a widespread outage, you may need to wait for Ancestry to resolve the problem.

If the status page shows normal operation but Ancestry is still not working, the issue is likely on your end. The following troubleshooting steps will help identify and resolve the problem.

IP Address Blocking Issues
==========================

If Ancestry displays "pretty-print { }" instead of loading the page, your IP address may have been blocked [citation:1]. This can occur even without using a VPN, and it may persist across multiple browsers and devices.

To resolve this issue, try accessing Ancestry via a VPN. The free VPN built into Firefox browser can be a simple solution [citation:1]. Alternatively, try connecting from a different network, such as mobile data or a different Wi-Fi network. Changing your public IP address often resolves the issue [citation:1].

Browser Troubleshooting
=======================

If Ancestry is not working in your web browser, the issue is often browser-related. Ancestry recommends using supported browsers for the best experience. On computers, Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox are recommended [citation:6]. On mobile devices, Safari (for iPhones and iPads) and Google Chrome (for iPhones, iPads, and Android) are recommended [citation:6].

**Update Your Browser**

Ensure your browser is up to date. Outdated browsers may have reduced functionality or may not work at all with Ancestry [citation:6]. Go to your browser's settings to find your current version and the option to update.

**Clear Cache and Cookies**

Clearing your browser's cache and cookies resolves many display and loading issues [citation:5][citation:6]. If your cache and cookies have not been cleared recently, your browser may try to load a previous version of a page rather than the current one [citation:5]. After clearing, close the browser completely and open it again.

**Disable Browser Extensions**

Browser extensions, including ad blockers and privacy tools, can interfere with Ancestry [citation:5][citation:6]. Try temporarily disabling all extensions to see if Ancestry works. If it does, re-enable extensions one by one to identify the culprit.

**Adjust Privacy and Security Settings**

If your browser's privacy and security settings are too high or strict, they can prevent Ancestry pages from displaying correctly [citation:5]. Try temporarily adjusting these settings or turning off Enhanced Tracking Protection for Ancestry.

Mobile App Troubleshooting
==========================

If the Ancestry mobile app is not working, the problem may be app-specific. The app may crash, freeze, fail to open, or not display trees correctly [citation:2].

**Close and Restart the App**

Start by closing the app completely and reopening it. This simple step often resolves temporary issues [citation:2].

**Update the App**

Ensure you have the latest version of the Ancestry app. Ancestry periodically releases new versions to provide new features and fix issues [citation:2]. If you cannot update the app, check your device for system updates.

**Restart Your Device**

Restarting your phone or tablet can clear temporary system issues that may be causing the app to malfunction [citation:2].

**Uninstall and Reinstall the App**

If the app continues to have problems, uninstall and reinstall the app [citation:2]. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device but does not erase your data, which is stored on the Ancestry website. After reinstalling, sign in to your account again.

**Device Compatibility**

If you see a message that the Ancestry app is not compatible with your device, first check for device updates [citation:7][citation:8]. If your device is up to date and you still see that message, the app will not install on that device. In that case, try accessing Ancestry from a recommended browser instead of from the app [citation:7][citation:8].

**System Requirements**

The Ancestry app requires iOS 15.0 or higher and Android 7.0 or higher [citation:2]. If your device is older and not supported, consider switching to a supported device or using the Ancestry website.

Security Software and VPNs
==========================

Security software, including antivirus programs, firewalls, internet security suites, and VPNs, may block content Ancestry needs to work properly [citation:4]. If parts of Ancestry are not loading correctly, your membership is not being recognized, or you are seeing unexpected errors, security software may be the cause.

**Temporarily Disable Security Software**

To determine whether security software is causing the issue, temporarily turn it off, test Ancestry, and then turn it back on [citation:4]. On a computer, access your security software from the menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows). On a mobile device, temporarily turn off any VPN or security app and reopen the Ancestry app or refresh the browser [citation:4].

**Add Ancestry to the Allowed List**

If Ancestry works while your security software is disabled, add Ancestry to the allowed or trusted list in your security software [citation:4]. You may need to allow the following domains:

- *.ancestry.com
- *.ancestrycdn.com
- *.search.ancestry.com
- *.purchase.ancestry.com
- *.pointofsale.ancestry.com

Login Problems
==============

If you cannot sign in to Ancestry, try the following steps [citation:2][citation:7][citation:8]:

- Verify your email address and password are correct.
- Use the "Forgot password" link to reset your password if needed.
- Try signing in through a recommended browser. If you can sign in through the browser but not the app, uninstall and reinstall the app.
- If you cannot sign in using either the app or a browser, reset your password.

Other Issues
============

**Pages Not Saving**

If pages are not saving or buttons are not working, ensure you are using a recommended browser and clear your cache and cookies [citation:6]. Disable browser extensions and temporarily disable security software.

**404 Errors**

If you are getting a 404 error, try navigating to the page from a different page on the site rather than using a bookmark or favorite [citation:6]. If this works, replace your bookmark with the current page URL.

**Slow Loading**

If pages are loading slowly, check your internet connection and clear your cache and cookies. Ensure your browser is up to date and consider disabling extensions that may be slowing performance.

Conclusion
==========

Ancestry not working is a common issue that can usually be resolved through systematic troubleshooting. By checking for service outages, addressing IP address blocking, troubleshooting your browser or mobile app, and adjusting security software settings, you can restore access to your family history research. If none of these steps resolve the issue, contact Ancestry support for further assistance.
